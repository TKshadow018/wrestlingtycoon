# People Guides

Use these guides to understand what each role does and how to get value from its abilities. Wrestlers and the support roles are hired through the employee market; board members are part of the promotion's governance system and are not ordinary hires.

- [Wrestler](WRESTLER.md) — ring abilities, popularity, stamina, and booking
- [Staff](STAFF.md) — department assignments and operational support
- [Manager](MANAGER.md) — wrestler representation, promos, interviews, and factions
- [Announcer](ANNOUNCER.md) — show presentation and segment effects
- [Referee](REFEREE.md) — match officiating and match-rating support
- [Board Member](BOARD_MEMBER.md) — monthly decisions, votes, Relation, and mandates

For shared hiring, contracts, titles, and story systems, see [Roster and Storylines](../ROSTER_AND_STORYLINES.md). For a start-to-finish walkthrough, see the [Player Guide](../GAMER_GUIDE.md).
