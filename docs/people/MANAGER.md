# Managers

Managers represent wrestlers and help with character and story segments. They are optional hires and are not counted as wrestlers for event staffing requirements.

## Abilities

The manager skill score averages charisma, negotiation, promo skill, strategy, and popularity. For the implemented segment effect, charisma and promo skill matter: when a manager appears in an interview or promo with a wrestler assigned to them, the manager can add up to two fans to that segment.

## Assign and book

1. In Manage Employees, assign the manager to a wrestler.
2. Add that manager and the assigned wrestler together in an interview or promo segment.
3. Check the event result to see which support contributions were applied.

Assigning a manager by itself does not add fans. A manager cannot be booked as a match wrestler, but managers can appear in promos and interviews, join factions, and take part in faction story beats.

Managers are support employees for payroll purposes: participating in an event incurs their appearance fee, and repeated segment use may add a heavy-usage bonus. Monthly salary and event appearance costs are separate. A manager's negotiation or strategy attributes contribute to the displayed overall skill score; do not assume they produce a gameplay effect beyond those explicitly used by the game.
