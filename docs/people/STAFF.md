# General Staff

General staff support the promotion behind the scenes. At least one staff member is required to run an event. Their main gameplay value comes from a department assignment in Manage Employees, not from being placed on a match card.

## Choose and assign a department

Each staff member can be assigned to one department. Compare the relevant ability before assigning them:

| Department | Useful ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Production | Technical skill | Reduces event production costs by 5% per staff member at 100 technical skill, up to a 10% total cap. |
| Medical | Technical skill | Reduces match-injury recovery duration by 10% per staff member at 100 technical skill, up to a 15% cap. It does not prevent injury or reduce recovery below seven days. |
| Talent Relations | Teamwork | Each employee covers `ceil(teamwork / 50)` of the lowest-happiness wrestlers, up to two per employee and four overall. Covered wrestlers can gain one happiness per day. |
| Logistics | Reliability | Reduces daily operating costs by 5% per staff member at 100 reliability, up to a 10% total cap. |

Abilities contribute proportionally below 100, and each department effect has a fixed cap. Assignments are useful even when a staff member does not appear on the show.

## Hiring and upkeep

Staff skill is derived from reliability, pressure handling, teamwork, technical skill, and popularity. Happiness is tracked for general staff; low happiness can lead to demands or departure. Staff have negotiated monthly salary and appearance-fee terms. The monthly salary is a recurring contract cost; support-employee appearance fees apply when they participate in event segments.
