# Board Members

Board members represent the promotion's shareholders and govern the business. They are not hires from the employee market, are not part of the show staffing minimums, and do not take part in wrestling segments.

## Board and officers

The game has seven configured board members whose shareholdings total 100%. Each has a board role, such as Chair, Vice Chair, committee chair, or Director. The Board tab shows the board, current officers, Relation, meeting history, mandates, and the next meeting.

January meetings include an election for President and Vice President. The General Manager is not an election candidate. Board members are also associated with agenda topics and may present them during meetings.

## Monthly meetings and decisions

Board meetings are held monthly on the first available day on or after the first of the month. At a meeting, review the operating-period finance report and resolve three discussion topics. Depending on the current game state, topics can cover finance, sponsors, events, titles, wrestler pushes, investments, competitors, or mandates.

Some proposals require a vote. Votes are weighted by shareholding, and a proposal needs more than 50% support to pass. Decisions can have a cash cost, change Board Relation, or create a mandate; check these consequences before choosing.

## Relation and business risk

Board Relation starts at 60 and ranges from 0 to 100. Low Relation can increase dismissal risk. Three consecutive negative operating periods cause deterministic dismissal and end the game, so monitor the meeting finance report and respond to recovery requests.

Board decisions do not replace show management. You still hire employees, book and run events, and manage daily finances. For agendas, vote rules, mandates, and dismissal details, see the [Board Meeting System](../BOARD_MEETING_PLAN.md).
